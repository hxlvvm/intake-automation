# Clinical review before booking (version 1)

Synthetic practice policy for this demo.

- Anyone who mentions an eating disorder is reviewed by a registered dietitian before a slot is offered, so the
  right specialist and level of care are chosen. The intake team does not book these directly.
- Patients under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent on file before booking.
